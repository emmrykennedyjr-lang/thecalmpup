Will this fix my dog's anxiety?

Honest answer: these are comfort and enrichment tools, not medicine. Many dogs settle noticeably with a safe bed, gentle pressure, and something to do — but severe separation anxiety deserves a chat with your vet or a certified trainer. That's also why we have the 30-day guarantee.

What's in the Calm Daily Chews, and are they safe?

Each soft chew contains L-theanine, chamomile and thiamine (vitamin B1) in a chicken-flavored base, made in a US facility. The exact amounts are printed on the label. They're a supplement, not medicine. Start with half the label dose for the first few days. If your dog is pregnant, nursing, on medication, or has a health condition, ask your vet before starting. For severe anxiety, talk to your vet: prescription options exist and work.

How does the chew subscription work?

A fresh tub ships every 30 days at 15% off. Skip a month, change the date, or cancel from your account or by replying to any email. No fees, no phone calls.

How long does shipping take?

Calming Chews ship from the US and typically arrive in 3–6 business days. Beds, shirts and toys are processed in 1–3 business days and typically arrive in 7–12 business days. Big orders may arrive in two packages. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as it ships.

Which bed size do I need?

Measure your dog nose-to-tail while lying down and add 6 inches. Small: up to 25 lb · Medium: 25–45 lb · Large: 45–80 lb · XL: 80+ lb. Between sizes? Go up — anxious dogs love to sink in.

How does the calming shirt fit?

It should be snug like a hug, not tight. Measure around the widest part of the chest. You should be able to slide two fingers under it.

What if my dog destroys it?

Inside 30 days, it's covered — email us a photo. The puzzle ball is built for heavy chewers, but no toy is indestructible, so supervise the first few sessions.

How do returns work?

Email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number. We'll refund you — usually no need to ship anything back.
